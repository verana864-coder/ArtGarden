How It Works

Simple steps to make a lasting impact on a child's educational journey

1. Discover Artists

Step 1

Browse through profiles of talented young artists, read their stories, and explore their unique artwork collections. Each child has a dream and a story to share.

2. Support & Collect

Step 2

Support an artwork with just $5. Each piece needs 20 supporters to reach the $100 goal that funds the child's education for a month.

3. Download & Display

Step 3

Once an artwork is fully supported, download your high-resolution digital art and display it proudly. You now own a piece of a child's dream.
